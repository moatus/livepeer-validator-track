Four images can reveal a broad outlier—but the rule is fragile

Private CONSENSUS-007 tested four anonymous outputs for the same prompt: all SD1.4, or three SD1.4 plus one SD1.5 (close substitute) or SDXL (broad substitute). A JPEG75 control compressed one honest image. One RTX3090 simulated four operators; this was not a deployed network.

Forty fresh prompts produced 240 new attempts with unique independent seed streams: four SD1.4, one SD1.5 and one SDXL per prompt. Twenty prompts calibrated each frozen image representation; twenty were held out. One preserved safety block left 19 valid of 20 scheduled groups per condition. Conditions reuse images and are paired.

Each image’s score compares its nearest peer distance with distances inside the other trio. Only a unique maximum strictly exceeding the maximum of 20 honest calibration maxima flags a slot; ties abstain. RPA uses 1024-dimensional patch embeddings; PickScore uses image embeddings only. The rule receives numeric features, without model labels or text.

All counts use 19 valid groups. “Wrong” flags an honest slot in a substitution group. “False” flags a slot in an honest group. Honest abstentions: RPA 19, PickScore 17; JPEG abstentions: RPA 1, PickScore 18.

Positive signal: RPA correctly flagged the broad substitute in 14/19 groups and ranked it most unusual in 19/19. PickScore ranked it first in 15/19, but correctly flagged only 4/19. Rank-only choices are distinct from calibrated alarms.

Failures matter: neither method correctly flagged a close substitute. RPA falsely flagged the compressed honest role in 18/19 JPEG groups. PickScore’s one JPEG false flag selected an unmodified honest role. This does not support standalone model-identity enforcement or penalties.

Interpretation limits: RPA’s source pretraining included SDXL and SD1.5; removing its class head retains learned source features. Fresh pilot images were not used for local fitting, but unseen-generator generalization is unproven and exact upstream training overlap is unverified. SDXL was generated natively at 1024 then resized to 512; SD1.x was native 512, confounding resolution and configuration with model identity.

Small convenience sample; nominal calibration depends on exchangeability. Three complete five-job blocks out of four scheduled gave rank-only RPA close 2/3 and broad 3/3; PickScore close 1/3 and broad 3/3. These reuse the same jobs and are neither calibrated alarms nor independent confirmation.
