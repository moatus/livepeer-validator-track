Frozen method and uncertainty

For each image, score = minimum cosine distance to any peer divided by the median pairwise distance within the remaining trio, floored at 1e−12. Only a unique highest score strictly above its threshold flags a slot; ties abstain. Each embedding threshold is the maximum of 20 all-honest calibration maxima, nominal 1/21 ≈ 5% under exchangeability, not validated production false-alarm control. No substitute labels or heldout outcomes set thresholds. Thresholds and blind decisions were written before evaluation labels were opened. No combined OR rule.
