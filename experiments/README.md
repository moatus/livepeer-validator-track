# Experiments and reproducible material

- [`m2/network-snapshot/`](m2/network-snapshot/) — the shared, measured M2 network baseline, `livepeer-arbitrum-2026-10-09-v1.json`. One frozen artifact includes all 100 orchestrators, pinned operator-address stake, indexed delegation links, same-round inputs, 30-day redeemed fees and actual issuance, with source evidence and hashes embedded. Network-grounded M2 models load it through the reader and keep proposed rules and scenario assumptions separate. A readable view is generated from this same file.
- [`m1/baseline-accounting/`](m1/baseline-accounting/) — the M1.1 baseline accounting workbook. `build.py` generates a spreadsheet that reproduces the litepaper's reward arithmetic for one round and two nodes: fee split, emission shares, MFS (the rule that caps a node's reward share at its stake share) and the validator multiplier. `verify.py` checks the workbook against independent arithmetic fixtures. The numbers are illustrative, not an emissions policy or a forecast; the research workbench cites the results as its baseline workbook.
- [`m2/self-funded-return/`](m2/self-funded-return/) — EX-03, the historical September M2.2 return calculation for one additional self-funded real job. `build.py` writes CSV and Markdown tables for both readings of the node reward and the emissions concepts C0–C3 under illustrative inputs; `verify.py` checks hand-computed fixtures and sign regions. Results show conditions, not forecasts, and say nothing about misconduct or what validators can detect.
- [`m2/evidence-self-funded/`](m2/evidence-self-funded/) — EX-04, the September-source M2.2 evidence comparison for a self-funded real job beside release testing on the operator's own node. Tolerances were committed before any outcome; synthetic case packets, a claim matrix and separation verdicts follow, and `verify.py` checks their structure. The packets test whether a judgment is supportable from stated records; they do not measure accuracy, effort or prevalence.

- [`m2/october-reconciliation/`](m2/october-reconciliation/) — EX-05, the October bridge from the historical pilot to scheduled emissions and unminted node allowances using the shared measured snapshot. Scenario choices and ownership proxies remain assumptions.
- **Planned:** EX-06 at `m2/capability-pricing/`, the connected scenario ledger defined in [Slice 2](../docs/m2/SLICE-02.md), with focused evidence at `m2/evidence-capability-pricing/`. Preparation only; no Slice 2 results yet.

For each experiment, identify its task and question; input versions and access or licensing constraints; method; expected controls; execution instructions; resource or paid-call budget; result artifact; errors and limitations. Separate simulations from measured network observations.

Use bounded fixtures or existing authorized services. Do not upload credentials, customer payloads or unrelated datasets. Model-runner deployment is not a prerequisite for this coordination repository.
