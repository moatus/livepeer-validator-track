One-round modeling baseline

Use the matched round fees, stake and pool cuts from round_inputs(). The 30-day period and this completed round are different windows. The current round is incomplete and the reader rejects it as a completed-round baseline.

Current protocol active stake and current live pool stake also differ because their accounting times differ. The reader returns same-round stake shares with the matching denominator. Applying capture-time operator-address fractions to that round is an assumption a model must state.
