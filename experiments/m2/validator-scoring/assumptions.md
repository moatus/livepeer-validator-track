## Compensation and conditional utility

Scheduled ceiling C(r)=C0×2^(-r/840), LPT per earning round. C0 reuses EX-06/08's snapshot-based one-round actual-issuance calibration as an illustrative launch ceiling; it is not a measured future launch parameter. Node envelope .94C, validator envelope V=.01C, treasury .05C use paper-proposed appendix shares. Validator/treasury envelopes mint in full even when MFS/score/claim limits reduce node issuance. “Post-BME mint” is interpreted as the independent scheduled mint envelope, not .01 times realized node issuance. Starting fee-side validator receipts are zero at full burn. No fee-funded replacement or price appreciation is presumed.

Base compensation split is appendix stake-weighted, using total self-plus-delegated bonded stake behind active seats as the explicit interpretation of “delegated stake”; alternative equal-per-seat split is a single algebraic control. Let b_i be that bonded stake (LPT); a_i=b_i or 1 respectively. Base eligibility e_i=1 for registered active i even when abstaining. The sole eligibility control sets e_i=1 only on a timely valid score, regardless of accuracy; no accuracy criterion is added. Gross receipt G_i=V e_i a_i / sum(e_j a_j). Eligibility and aggregation denominators are distinct. If no eligible weights exist, V still mints into an undistributed validator accounting balance; do not silently give it to nodes/treasury or burn it. With eligibility, splitting the full V among eligible recipients is an illustrative completion, not specified policy. Zero stake-weight denominator is undefined/needs rule, not a numeric zero receipt.

Validator reward cut k_i^v and owned delegator fraction w_i^v give retained fraction t_i^v=k_i^v+(1−k_i^v)w_i^v. Outside delegators receive G_i(1−k_i^v)(1−w_i^v). Base cuts/proxies reuse the ledger's native fractions, explicitly applying them to validator receipts as an assumption. Equal/stake split changes pay, never score-voting weight. Rank by total stake is not independent beneficial control.

For node j let B_j=.94C min(f_j/F,s_j/S), before score and with all 100 ledger pools in F,S. Node earned payout R_j=B_j m_j at the actual claim; pending amounts are not spendable income. Retained operator-plus-owned-delegator fraction is k_j+(1−k_j)w_j, with unrelated receipts separately reported. More generally x_ij is the fraction of node j's reward economically owned by focal participant i across operator/delegator roles; sum of disjoint ownership fractions must not exceed one. Use the ledger native own-node fractions for the declared baseline; express cross-node exposure symbolically, with no invented ownership estimates. Node reward exposure can favor either side of a scoring dispute; missed reward is not principal slashing.

All utility terms below are USDC-equivalent per decision/earning-round cohort. P_claim is declared USDC/LPT valuation, not cash from selling or a price forecast. Use the ledger's frozen valuation/burn price (distinct variables even if numerically equal). ETH settlements are replayed only through EX-06's indexed-USD-to-hypothetical-USDC assumption at 1:1; no ETH is a proposed payment. Let K_tool be shared engineering/publication cost in USDC per covered round, allocated alpha_i to participant i only if it bears that cost; report network K_tool separately, never charge every validator the whole amount. Private c_i(a) is interpretation/review/submission effort, including contested effort if applicable. o_i(a) is the separately defined opportunity/liquidity cost excluding the same hours already in c. Time h can be converted only with declared wage v, c=h×v; both stay unknown here. Tooling availability never makes private review automatically free.

Define Z_i(a) as signed residual business/network-value/standing/retaliation/coordination consequences, USDC-equivalent, conditional and unmeasured; show its components separately if discussed, with no guaranteed sanction, reputation value or coordination benefit. U_i(a)=P_claim[G_i(a)t_i^v + sum_j x_ij R_j(a)] − c_i(a) − o_i(a) − alpha_i(a)K_tool + Z_i(a), plus action-invariant cash receipts/costs which cancel in paired differences. For finite multi-round interpretation, sum only the declared earning cohorts; do not mix a per-case private cost with all future compensation. Same-role comparison ΔU(a,b) holds other submissions, evidence, fees/stakes and participation fixed, varying only focal action. Prefer a only if ΔU(a,b)>=0 for each compared b, conditional on unknown terms; report ties. If a condition needs a sign of Z or unknown effort, leave it as a threshold inequality, not a measured sign.

Participation is a separate outside option: declining the role yields no validator receipt, retains node/delegator exposure under the replacement scores, avoids private validator costs and may change standing Z. ΔU(join,a versus decline)=P_claim[retained G_i(a)+sum_j x_ij ΔR_j]−incremental private/opportunity/tool costs+ΔZ. The pass-down replacement's score is stipulated evidence-following in the cooperative control only; otherwise unknown. Derive affordable private cost/valuation thresholds symbolically. Compensation at 840 and 1680 shrinks in LPT; participation need not shrink if costs, fees or valuation change. No participation rate, error rate or incidence is assigned.
