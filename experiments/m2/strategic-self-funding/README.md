# EX-08: strategic self-funding across network conditions

Status: **Drafted finite core accounting; supplementary calculations pending.** This implements the first 69 profiles of the finite comparison in [SLICE-02B.md v1.2](../../../docs/m2/SLICE-02B.md). It asks how a single fixed-stake operator's real controlled purchases change retained value and honest-party rewards across structures, outside business and market scales. Frozen input reconstruction covers all 135 selections; computed outcomes cover 54 core profiles, three fee-leaning confirmations, nine aligned controls and three native measured references. Supplementary outcomes, cohort timelines and the evidence-scope table remain uncomputed.

The comparison starts with cooperative outside service, keeps a matched useful-internal reference beside each choice, and then introduces ordinary demand/routing responses and strategic purchases. Strategic reward-only purpose is known only to the modeler; the selected behavior is stipulated dishonest for the scenario. This premise supplies neither validator evidence nor an established eligibility policy. Payment, execution, quality, availability, independent demand, economic value and dishonesty remain distinct. Common control, price, profit or capability uniqueness alone establishes no violation. The reward-objective criterion remains VD-19.

## Whole-network construction and precision

Sort source pools by same-round stake descending, breaking ties by address. Source ranks and address keys remain stable, displayed as anonymous modeled roles. Actors are ranks 1, 50 and 90, selected before outcomes. R retains measured stake and raw-fee distribution including zero-fee pools; D uses stake (101−k)/5050 and fee weight k/5050; C uses .20, nine .04 shares and .44(101−k)/4095 at ranks 11–100 with the same pre-override fee weights as D. D/C replace both source distributions and are illustrative stress fixtures.

For low/high business, a=.25s/.90s replaces the actor fee share. Each nonactor's parent weight is multiplied by (1−a)/(1−w_actor). Every vector contains parent, assigned and actual outside fee shares, the complete fee reallocation differences, source keys, stakes, native proxies, outside jobs, prices, costs, capacities and timing. D versus C therefore includes mechanically required fee reallocation alongside stake changes; it is not a pure causal stake effect. Aligned controls use fee shares equal to stakes with no headroom override. Native references use measured fees, stake, cuts and address ownership with no artificial headroom or retention override.

All arithmetic uses Decimal precision 60 and ROUND_HALF_EVEN. Normalized nonactor shares quantize to 1e−48; the actor's exact h×s is preserved. Constructed fee/stake amounts quantize to 1e−42. Native R stake amounts remain exact. Stake residual goes to source rank 100. Fee residual goes to the lowest-ranked positive pre-override fee-weight nonactor, recorded in each state; zero-fee source pools remain zero. Assigned shares describe construction; actual shares are recomputed from conserved monetary amounts. Tiny quantization differences are explicit and below the verifier's 1e−18 agreement tolerance.

Hashed bytes are sorted-key compact UTF-8 JSON, ensure_ascii=False, no terminal newline; Decimals are fixed-point strings with trailing fractional zeroes removed, no exponent, zero `0`. Hash scope is the full state object and its ordered 100-pool vector. Generated files add one newline, excluded from the hash. No binary float enters construction. Source and contract hashes are checked before materialization; vector hashes are checked before generated files are written. A changed contract requires an explicit reviewed revision, not a silent hash refresh.

## Accounting adapter and continuous optimization

[adapter.py](adapter.py) maps `source_key` to EX-06 pool `address`, with `stake,k,d,w,score,claim_at,caps` passed as Decimal/int data. `caps` uses `generic` and optional `niche`, each with q_out, q_self, p, c, setup, u and capacity. The frozen no-action caps have q_self/setup/u zero. Positive action populates only the declared controlled offer with x/p jobs and its setup; useful pairs change only u to 1. Every ledger calls unchanged `account(pools, ceiling, valuation, burn_price, horizon=7, reading='mfs', node=.94, validator=.01, treasury=.05, node_fee=.5)`. Its setup is unconditional once supplied, so the adapter supplies zero setup at x=0. Appendix is supported only at full scores. Source, dependency, contract and exact vector hashes are checked before accounting. No generated table supplies an economic input.

For stake-acquisition arithmetic, use dS=(a S−S_i)/(1−a) only at actual a≥s: equality gives zero, an above-cap position gives a positive identity, and below-cap/high-business a=.90s positions are not applicable. No negative stake purchase is inferred. The §7 high-business parenthetical is a wording erratum, with no selection, formula or bound change.

The diagnostic contract is frozen in `inputs.json.phase2_contract.hypothetical_diagnostic_adapter`. Only labelled hypothetical capacity-infeasible diagnostics (including beyond-capacity x=F, cap boundaries, unconstrained optima and landmarks) use an engine-call-only copy with per-offer capacity max(declared capacity, stipulated q_out+q_self). Original inputs remain unchanged. The reported rows restore declared per-offer capacities; temporary capacity is never displayed as service capacity. Feasible optima/endpoints and capacity-feasible cash-infeasible diagnostics pass declared capacities unchanged. Independent exact inclusive cash and capacity checks remain separate because account() checks no cash bounds. Original EX-06 replay and verification are a live precondition before diagnostic use. The label “hypothetical execution beyond declared capacity” establishes no affordability, observed spare capacity or feasible conduct.

[strategy.py](strategy.py) locates zero, cap kink, eligible stationary point `sqrt(P*.94E*t*(F-f)/L)-F` and the feasible endpoint, with `L=1-.5z+c/p`. [build.py](build.py) compares their payoffs through EX-06, rather than a sampled grid. The feasible interval is `[0,min((B-setup)/L,p Qmax)]`, with setup charged only for positive action. Zero action remains eligible. Positive setup uses piecewise quadratic break-even roots; zero setup uses the rational uncapped root or the post-cap linear root. Collective honest-reward loss roots solve the monotone piecewise sum across victim cap crossings; individual roots distinguish initial fee limitation from stake protection. Every reported landmark is checked through the engine.

The builder reports at most eight exactly deduplicated progression points per profile. Individual 5/10/20% landmarks and collective 5/10/20% landmarks are distinct. Exact inclusive cash and capacity checks label affordability, ordering relative to the actor optimum/cap and the capacity endpoint. Fee-limited 20% landmarks preserve `.25F` exactly. Decimal division can round a cash endpoint outward; the implementation moves it inward by one representable step when necessary, without increasing B. The 1e−18 tolerance checks arithmetic agreement only.

Aligned controls retain the frozen declared equality and zero reward headroom. Actual monetary fee/stake shares and their tiny quantization residual remain in outputs. That structural equality sets the cap boundary and added-stake identity to zero; it never extends cash or capacity. All other optima use actual monetary shares. Added-stake arithmetic applies only to actually capped positions and is not a modeled purchase.

Every point retains all 100 pools, their baseline/changed rewards, absolute/relative losses, outside receipts, retained income, external-delegator effects and fee/stake-limit transitions. Scheduled allowance, claimability, actual mint, permanent MFS non-minting, score withholding, pending claims, full validator/treasury envelopes, buyback, burn and net supply remain separate. Actor gain is split between changed unminted allowance and peer loss; a negative allowance release means allowance becomes unavailable again, and gain-source shares are undefined when actor gain is zero. No validator or treasury income is assigned to the actor.

Net value includes unsold tokens at the stated price; realized incremental cash stays separate. Useful references add only declared use value. Capped-position added stake, E2, P1, evidence validity, practical capacity, delegation movement and studies 3–8 remain unresolved or pending.

## Core results and counterexamples

Under the illustrative full-retention actor, p=1, c=.30, full scores and declared one-round B=.10F, all 54 below-cap core profiles have a positive feasible incremental valued return. Forty-seven stop at the stake cap, four at the budget convention and three at an interior stationary point in the near-parity market. Capacity is checked and does not bind any profitable optimum. These are conditional worked profiles, not observed networks or prevalence.

All nine aligned controls and three fee-leaning confirmations choose zero. At aligned shares, more spending cannot increase the fixed-stake reward. At reward/fee ratio .5, the derivative is already negative before a cap. The identity establishes the same zero sign for the other standardized fee-leaning profiles without calculating additional rows. Larger harm-only diagnostics are explicitly separate from profitable stopping points.
