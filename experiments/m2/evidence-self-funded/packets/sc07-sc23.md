# sc07-sc23: evidence before the first reward claim

## Allegations, before evidence

**Narrow (I-20):** the allegation is confined to the explicitly labelled SC-07 purpose-misrepresentation variant in `tolerances.md`. The operator knowingly calls reward-only jobs useful release testing to retain full inflation eligibility, although no productive or testing use accounts for them. Representation, falsity, knowledge and eligibility purpose each need support. Unmodified SC-07 has accurate service records and no pretence; it contains no specified narrow conduct. The narrow matrix's SC-07 rows denote the variant, never a silent change to the original case.

**Broad (I-21):** the allegation concerns funding jobs at a commonly controlled node for the resulting inflation reward, with no productive or release-testing use accounting for them. All these elements matter. Profitable useful testing remains the legitimate mixed-purpose boundary. Common ownership, an unused output and reward responsiveness alone establish neither allegation.

These are possible criteria, not paper-approved rules (SRC-LP20 L47, L86; VD-09). Whether the distinction is needed for the contribution the reward is meant to encourage remains I-01 and GT-03's question.

## Controls and hidden case facts

All contents and arrival times below are synthetic assumptions. The exercise starts with competent reviewers and accurate payment, execution and promised-result checks. It assumes trustworthy common-control information, rather than deriving control from a wallet address. It does not assume a deployed evidence feed, universal access or a duty to answer. The paper proposes published shared investigation (SRC-LP20 L88), not the specific records below.

Read SC-23 first: its operator uses real jobs to test a release's billing path and then adopts the tested configuration in the release. The jobs can also earn rewards. Next read SC-07: the organization funds correctly executed jobs only for rewards, without productive or release-testing use. These purpose facts are experimental ground truth, not validator observations. In the narrow variant only, add the knowingly false release-testing representation. Hidden truth must not fill an unknown matrix cell.

The first claim is at paper-proposed n+7, using the median current at claim (SRC-LP20 L109, L224). No scoring participation, score severity, public hearing, appeal or later recovery is assumed. A lower individual score is not guaranteed to lower the median. Consequences below are conditional on the relevant scores changing that median.

## Claim judgments at n+7

`matrix.csv` contains the six prescribed claims for each case, standard and evidence level. Its values describe evidence, so they are shared by both I-22 readings. The interpretation changes the consequence, not an unknown's truth value.

- P1 establishes CL-01 (settlement). P2 establishes CL-02 (execution/delivery) and CL-03 (correctness), solely within the controlled observations.
- P0 contradicts CL-05 (independent demand) for both commonly controlled purchases. Independent customers are not thereby an eligibility requirement.
- CL-06 (economic value of the subsidy) remains unknown everywhere. Useful testing and a subsequent operational change do not price the benefit to the network or establish that it is worth the inflation paid.
- CL-07 is unknown for both cases at the public level, under both standards. P3 is an assertion, not a check of its truth. At public+disclosed, D1 contradicts the selected dishonesty allegation against SC-23: it supports the stated release use and preserves useful mixed-purpose testing. SC-07 remains unknown, including falsity and knowledge in the narrow variant. Silence is not evidence of those elements.

These CL-07 judgments address the selected allegation, not every possible dishonest act by an operator. The unmodified SC-07 contains no specified narrow act; assigning it a narrow cut simply for silence would be a false cut under the committed tolerance.

## Social burden and limits

Validators or shared investigators raise Q; the operator's engineers control D1. Reading (a) permits non-disclosure with an unresolved allegation. Reading (b) makes the bounded explanation a practical condition for avoiding a possible cut in this inquiry. Legitimate work can therefore face preparation effort, release metadata exposure and income uncertainty even where the final decision is correct. The paper's intended shared tooling and mutual accountability can help, but neither provides the missing source automatically (SRC-LP20 L83, L88).

Claim delay supplies time; here the assumed n+5 disclosure can affect the n+7 judgment. Elapsed time does not establish missing purpose evidence. If a lower median results, its reach is the node's eligible round reward, not just the tested job; fee repayment, principal slashing and payment to an accuser are not assumed (I-19). This exercise does not calculate return or settle the reward objective. It tests logical support in one synthetic pair, not detection accuracy, review effort, access, prevalence, dispute incidence or validator incentives. Those costs and incentives require later inquiry.
