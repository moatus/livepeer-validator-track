# E2 prospective tolerances

## Allegations and readings

These qualitative research criteria precede all E2 outcome packets, claim matrices and verdicts. They adapt EX-04's error, effort and disclosure distinctions to correctness and operator response. They are neither empirical rates nor approved scoring policy. October authority: [paper](../../../docs/litepaper-2.0.md), PDF p4 L193–208, p5 L218–221, p6 L271–278. September EX-04 locators remain historical.

The M2 research premise from [DECISIONS](../../../docs/DECISIONS.md) is that rewards are owed to real, correctly executed, honestly represented paid work, and buying one's own work solely to raise reward share is dishonest. Common ownership and reward responsiveness alone are not violations. The stricter requirement of contribution beyond paid work is conditional only where it changes eligibility; payment or correct execution cannot establish that additional contribution.

**K, correctness allegation.** The node served a delivered result outside a task term it accepted, rather than another permitted draw. The selected test term and its authentication requirements are fixed in [reconstruction.md](reconstruction.md): a valid PNG with exactly illustrative 512 by 512 decoded pixels. Neither artistic preference, output inequality, ordinary coverage limits nor model availability establishes this allegation. No general semantic quality test or uptime grade is implied. A breach establishes the selected incorrect-result predicate only. If a judgment reading additionally requires knowing misrepresentation, evidence of knowledge is required separately; dimension mismatch alone cannot supply it.

**P, inherited purpose allegation.** Preserve EX-04/EX-07's narrow explicitly labelled knowing purpose-misrepresentation variant and broad circular funding, reward-only purpose and absence of productive/testing use. Unmodified accurate real self-payment has no narrow deception by definition. SC-06's useful affiliate production is not misconduct by ownership alone. E2 will assess inheritance conditions, without a new purpose matrix. A settlement spike is a trigger for a question, not an allegation proven by its shape.

**R, response question.** Compare an operator's ability to answer the same allegation with the same merits evidence. Notice failure, lack of reasons, late response or unsafe disclosure is not itself evidence of K or P. Reading **a (evidence required)** permits an adverse finding only on sufficient obtainable evidence of the specified violation; otherwise retain unknown. Reading **b (explanation burden)** permits a procedural cut for non-explanation within a declared opportunity; it must be reported separately from evidentiary separation and can exceed false-cut tolerance. The paper specifies neither this duty nor a guaranteed response channel.

## Individual error tolerances

Apply each row separately to K and the inherited P standards, at public and public-plus-disclosed evidence levels, and to both response paths. Do not average tolerances across cases.

| ID | Reading | Prospective limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T-FC-a | a | Strict: no claim-time reward cut for a nonviolating result, legitimate variation, useful production/testing, common ownership, missing private evidence or inability to answer. Adverse evidence must support the selected allegation with attribution and authentication. |
| T-MV-a | a | Unknown is accepted when necessary evidence cannot be obtained within the limits, and must be reported as an inquiry limitation. Ignoring obtainable evidence that establishes the violation is unacceptable. Unknown is neither innocence nor a successful defense. |
| T-FC-b | b | Strict at first claim: a procedural cut on nonviolating work exceeds tolerance, including when the operator lacks notice, safe disclosure or timely access. An earlier cut followed by a supported preclaim correction can meet claim-time protection; do not erase interim burden. |
| T-MV-b | b | An established violation receiving full reward exceeds tolerance. A silence-based cut on an unknown case does not demonstrate detection. Preserve uncertainty even when the procedure permits a cut. |

A false cut reduces the claim-time reward of work that does not violate the selected standard. A missed violation leaves violating work without an adverse consequence by the first claim. Case-author knowledge can label a synthetic error for analysis, but cannot establish a validator claim. Strict false-cut tolerance is chosen because the median can reach the whole node's eligible round reward, including unrelated jobs and delegator receipts. No empirical zero-error guarantee, compensating redistribution, score magnitude, principal loss or automatic later recovery is assumed.

## Effort and disclosure ceilings

**T-ER, routine.** One pass over the supplied public records, their source, linkage, authenticity and arrival, applying the one declared file-format/dimension check where its inputs are available. Keep CL-01 payment, CL-02 execution/delivery, CL-03 correctness, CL-04 availability, CL-05 independent demand, CL-06 subsidy value and CL-07 selected dishonesty separate.

**T-EC, contested.** Routine review plus one focused question and one response bundle, containing relevant accepted terms, input/output digests, delivery acknowledgment and bounded verification report; for inherited purpose, use only the inherited plan/use bundle. Allow one correction of an identified transcription or attribution error within that same exchange. Repeated demands, unrelated-job audits, forensic ownership tracing, third-party interviews, new model training or replaying an entire private workflow exceed tolerance. No minutes, dollars or demonstrated feasibility are assigned. A shared published investigation may reduce duplicated collection/checking, conditional on trustworthy usable evidence; it does not guarantee access or scorer action.

**T-DP, disclosure.** At most one selectively disclosed redacted job bundle, limited to terms, necessary job references/timing and an operator-authorized non-sensitive test output or authenticated bounded observation. Customer payloads, credentials, business accounts, unrelated histories, unrestricted code and full production roadmaps are outside tolerance. A private production output needed for the dimension check is not assumed safe to publish. If a digest cannot substitute for inspection and safe access is missing, preserve the limitation. Redaction must retain decisive linkage; a bare digest authenticates nothing without a trusted binding. No paper-provided confidential channel or access right is assumed.

For a, the response is voluntary; for b, the same ceiling bounds the demanded explanation. Declining unsafe disclosure is not proof of wrongdoing. Record operator preparation, metadata exposure, income uncertainty and access to reasons separately from correctness of the final finding. Delegators may bear uncertain rewards and capital immobility under the paper's proposed lock; neither principal slashing nor immediate escape is inferred. Validators/investigators also bear collection and interpretation effort. A corrected payout does not erase those burdens. Compare resources/access explicitly, without assigning favorable treatment by standing or bad faith.

## Timing tolerance

**T-T, first-claim reach.** Assess protection at illustrative earning round n's paper-proposed earliest claim n+7, using the median then current. Distinguish job time, redemption, evidence availability, notice, response, correction and score submission. Proposed illustrative schedule: public records n+1, focused notice/reasons n+2, adequate response n+5, possible correction n+6, earliest claim n+7. These are assumptions to test, not service guarantees or elapsed-day conversions. A late/unavailable/unsafe response path must preserve the same merits question. Late evidence cannot establish protection at the earlier claim; any later-claim analysis must state its actual median and the unresolved persistence/finality rule. Timely adequate evidence is not proof that enough validators update scores before claim.

## Limits

No E2 outcomes are contained here. Synthetic cases can test logical support and conditional burden, not practical access, error frequency, review time, prevalence or incentives. Every ceiling must be assessed individually later. If no sufficient case survives these criteria, report the unmet input rather than relax the criteria after outcomes. Detailed procedure design remains M4.3; practical access and correctness exercises go to M4.2; scorer incentives and the two conditioned evidence inputs go to M2.9.
