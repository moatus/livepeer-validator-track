# E2 criteria and source reconstruction

Status: prospective, no E2 outcomes. Read with [tolerances.md](tolerances.md) and [input-manifest.md](input-manifest.md). The decision is whether specified evidence obtainable before claim supports an adverse finding and whether an operator can answer within declared error, effort, privacy and timing limits. The M2 research premise is real, correctly executed, honestly represented paid work, with reward-only self-purchase treated as dishonest; it is not protocol policy. A requirement beyond paid work remains a conditional eligibility branch.

## Concrete correctness criterion, K1

Use an operator-authorized synthetic text-to-image job, with no customer payload. Before revealing results, fix the accepted task term: deliver one valid PNG with exactly illustrative 512 by 512 decoded pixels. The prompt, task type and accepted terms must identify the same job and node. No promise of an exact image, seed reproducibility, aesthetic score or semantic equivalence is added. Content, texture, composition and byte representation may vary; any draw satisfying the selected term is permitted for this predicate. Other task promises are outside this bounded test and remain unassessed, so passing K1 is not proof of complete CL-03 or CL-07 innocence.

Required observations: the contemporaneous offer and customer acceptance; the job identifier and accepted term; the delivered file bytes or a trustworthy independent inspection of those bytes; and a delivery acknowledgment binding the node/job and file digest. Pin input, terms and output digests, key identity and verification method. A signed offer must be shown to belong to the operator; a signed delivery receipt or authenticated endpoint transcript must bind the actual delivered digest to that accepted job. A customer assertion alone, unlinked screenshot, unsigned filename or repeat generation does not authenticate delivery. Signature validity establishes the signer's representation, not its truth; external custody/acknowledgment or a stated trusted observation is needed to resolve conflicting delivery claims. Attribute each check and custody step. Synthetic authentication can be stipulated, but must be labelled conditional practical access.

Feasible bounded check: inspect the file's PNG signature and IHDR width/height, verify chunk integrity, and decode it with a version-recorded image decoder; record the actual bytes' SHA-256, tool/version, dimensions, errors and check time. Prospective illustrative resource limits: one file no larger than 1 MiB, with decoder timeout 2 seconds and memory limit 256 MiB; record the later tool/version and enforce those limits before inspection. These are research ceilings, not measured effort. Failure within these bounds is not permission to enlarge them after seeing results; failure to check safely is unknown, not proof of incorrectness. A trusted independently repeatable inspection checks the same delivered bytes, not a new random draw. A geometry breach of an authenticated explicit accepted term can falsify that term while allowing different valid draws with the promised geometry. Decoder disagreement, ambiguous terms, altered bytes or broken linkage leaves this predicate unknown until resolved within the one exchange. This is a prospective test design, not an SC-10/11 verdict or a claim that the current protocol supplies these receipts.

For the paper's named example of serving incorrect results (October p4 L203–208), state whether the selected judgment treats an established term breach as sufficient for the specific adverse finding. If it requires intent/knowledge, carry that extra proof requirement; do not manufacture it from a malformed file. No score magnitude follows. CL-01 settlement and CL-02 execution/delivery need their own observations; K1 neither establishes independent demand, subsidy value nor broad availability. Transcoding verification code is evidence of a specific verification interface, not an arbitrary AI correctness oracle.

## Participant and record control

Independent customer: controls its funds, request and delivery acknowledgment; gateway controls routing/session logs; operator controls its offer, job handling and retained output records. Integrated payer/node: the organization may hold both sides, making independent custody an additional question, not evidence of misconduct. With outside delegators, the same organization cannot be assumed to control their stake or reward receipts. Delegators choose backing subject to the paper's proposed movement rules, and may face reward uncertainty without being holders of job evidence.

Validators submit individual scores; they do not collectively receive a new investigative power from this reconstruction. Shared investigators may collect and publish bounded evidence. Operators can supply a voluntary answer under reading a, or face a demanded explanation under b. Who sets proof criteria, sends notice, provides reasons, accepts corrections and reaches scorers is not specified by the paper. Mutual accountability and shared tooling are intended benefits, conditional on trustworthy evidence and access; they imply no guaranteed appeal, confidential channel, individual-score visibility, correction or favorable treatment by personal standing. Claim submission authority is unspecified. Do not infer beneficial control from an address.

## Four bounded comparisons and two response paths

Order cooperative legitimate service/variation before strategic allegations. SC-10 beside SC-11 receives one K1 tabletop, public then one expressly disclosed bundle. Record CL-01–CL-07 separately with established/contradicted/unknown states only after criteria are committed. Preserve EX-04's schema conventions; any correctness-specific standard/claim addition must be explicit, not a fabricated second purpose matrix.

SC-17 beside SC-16 receives only the frozen P1 raw redeemed-fee summaries and the October delay reading. Keep ETH, exact window and partial edges; use no new extraction, real-person misconduct claim or detection estimate. SC-06 beside SC-07 receives an inheritance disposition against EX-04 release testing and EX-07 useful production; check both rule and observation conditions before transfer. An affiliate's production is not identical to the operator's release testing, and an old disclosed release plan does not establish the new case's access or purpose.

For SC-25/26 hold the underlying allegation, merits evidence and criteria fixed. First plan a timely adequate one-bundle response through notice n+2, receipt n+5 and possible correction n+6; then plan a bounded late/unavailable/unsafe-disclosure response with the same underlying merits. Separate resource/access differences from differences in evidence and from scorers' action. Report preparation, business disclosure, income timing and delegator effects in both paths. Even an adequate response only permits a supported judgment; it does not guarantee a score update. Inability to furnish a response retains uncertainty under a; any b cut is a procedural consequence to assess against tolerance, never proof of the alleged violation. Do not assume an appeal route or a compensating benefit to accusers.

Shared investigation can publish one reusable authenticated check rather than require each validator to recover the same records. Test this as a conditional benefit beside residual operator disclosure and each scorer's interpretation effort. No quantified savings, duty to act or favorable median is assumed.

## Stop and handoff

Stop after four pair judgments/inheritance dispositions, six access assessments, both response-path burdens/outcomes and two conditioned Slice 3 evidence inputs. Seek an inconclusive input from existing purpose evidence and a sufficient input only if K1 supports it within the declared observation boundary. If no such sufficient input survives, report the unmet deliverable. The stricter contribution reading may leave eligibility unknown even with a supported correctness check; it does not change file geometry. E2 adds no economics, numeric scores or incentive finding. M2.9 tests action on evidence; M4.2 gets practical correctness/access and gateway/customer disclosure requests; M4.3 retains detailed procedure design. Reopen inherited results only for a logged material rule/evidence difference.
