# EX-07: tolerances for capability, pricing and self-funded traffic

## Alleged violations under each standard

**Narrow standard (I-20).** The allegation is knowingly making a false job-purpose representation to retain inflation eligibility. Use the explicitly labelled **S06-N** niche variant and **S08-N** discount variant only: at illustrative n+1 the operator represents, “These jobs are useful internal release tests”. The synthetic ground truth stipulates reward-only ordering, no productive or testing use, and the operator's knowing falsity. The claimed advantage is retaining full inflation eligibility while real payments raise the fee input. Representation, falsity, knowledge and eligibility purpose must each be examined separately; the stipulated truth is not an observation available to validators.

S05 and S07 make the same true useful-testing representation and may also earn rewards. Unmodified S06 and S08 contain no specified narrow conduct. The `-N` overlay adds only the explicit representation and its stipulated falsity, knowledge and purpose; it changes no price, cost, jobs or funding. Payment, execution and correctness records remain accurate. There is no fabricated payment or execution and no implicit deceptive act in the unmodified cases.

**Broad standard (I-21).** The allegation concerns circular funding of real jobs for rewards, with reward purpose the reason for the jobs and no productive or testing use accounting for them. Circular funding means the payer's funds support fees attributed to a commonly controlled node that can receive the associated reward; it does not mean the entire payment is recovered. Funding, reward-only purpose and absence of productive/testing use must all be addressed. Known common control, no credited use value, an unused output, ordinary discount routing or a conditional positive return alone does not establish this allegation. Useful production and testing, including mixed-purpose work that also earns rewards, remain legitimate references.

Both standards are possible interpretations, not adopted criteria. October names self-dealing among dishonesty examples but gives neither of these tests ([SRC-LP20-OCT, PDF p4, L203–208](../../../docs/litepaper-2.0.md)). Whether the contribution the reward is meant to encourage requires different treatment remains VD-19 and GT-03's separate question. No independent-customer requirement or blanket common-ownership restriction is imposed.

## Keyed adverse-judgment readings

I-22 carries two possible readings, applied to both standards and both evidence levels:

- **`a` — evidence required:** a cut needs evidence of the named violation. Unexplained jobs leave the relevant claim unknown; uncertainty alone is not a basis for an adverse finding.
- **`b` — non-explanation consequence:** failure to explain the jobs by n+7 may permit a procedural cut, placing the response burden on the operator. Examine that consequence even if it exceeds a tolerance below. Do not replace this reading with `a`, or treat a cut as proof of the violation.

The paper specifies neither this proof standard nor a disclosure duty (VD-09). Evidence separation is assessed independently of the permitted procedural consequence, which must retain its reading key and disclosure burden. Unknown does not become proof under either reading. A lower individual score need not change the median; any reward consequence is conditional on a change to the median applied at claim. No score severity or later correction is assumed.

A **false cut** reduces the claim-time reward of activity that does not violate the selected standard. A **missed violation** leaves a violating case without an adverse reward consequence by n+7. An unknown claim is not proof of either innocence or violation. These errors are separate from uncertainty about the accuracy of underlying records; their frequencies cannot be estimated from these synthetic comparisons.

## Legitimate references and bounded families

Read legitimate outside activity, then useful internal work, then the alleged case within each family:

- **Niche:** S04 useful outside purchases; S05 useful internal production/testing; S06 controlled no-use work, with S06-N separately labelled for the narrow allegation. A unique capability is not itself suspicious.
- **Discount:** S02 ordinary outside rerouting; S07 useful internal work on the discounted offer; S08 matched controlled no-use work, with S08-N separately labelled for the narrow allegation. A low price or cheapest route does not itself prove misconduct or independent demand.

S04 and S02 retain accurate route and funding explanations. Useful internal production and release testing remain protected references even when responsive to rewards. The shared scenario descriptions and hidden purpose premises do not automatically become validator observations. No real address or beneficial owner is identified. These families do not complete E2's original pairs.

## Arrival schedule and allowed disclosed sources

The contents, competent observations and access conditions are illustrative premises. The first-claim boundary is paper-proposed n+7, with the median current at claim ([SRC-LP20-OCT, PDF p6, L271–278](../../../docs/litepaper-2.0.md)); this is the inquiry's evidence cutoff, not a paper-stated disclosure deadline or finality rule (VD-11).

| Reviewer arrival | Allowed material and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Illustrative n+1 | Public listing, quote and payment records; stipulated, accurately disclosed control and route/funding explanations; the declared useful-testing representation in S05/S07 and the explicit S06-N/S08-N overlay. Control is disclosed, not inferred from addresses. The existence of a representation supplies no automatic evidence of its truth or the speaker's knowledge. |
| Illustrative n+2 | Task terms and authenticated execution/delivery and correctness observations linked to the jobs. Observation of one job is not evidence of general availability, independent demand or reward-only purpose. |
| Illustrative n+5 | At the disclosed level only, one voluntary, selectively redacted source bundle per family, as defined below. Its authenticity, access, adequacy, linkage, redaction and arrival are premises to check. Earlier source creation does not imply earlier reviewer access. |
| Paper-proposed n+7 | Close evidence for the first-claim comparison. S06/S08 and their labelled `-N` variants supply no productive/testing bundle by this boundary. **Niche source ceiling:** one linked production/test plan and output-use bundle, supplied voluntarily by the operator or engineers controlling it. Retain relevant job references, timing, the production/testing objective and linked output use. This is the allowed S05 internal explanation; S04 retains its accurate outside route/funding explanation.

**Discount source ceiling:** one selection-rule/routing-log bundle linked to the internal test plan, supplied voluntarily by the operator or engineers controlling it. Retain relevant job references, timing, route-selection grounds and the linked testing objective. This is the allowed S07 internal explanation; S02 retains its accurate outside route/funding explanation.

The **public** level contains only public arrivals and any focused question; **public+disclosed** adds the permitted family bundle. One focused question and one operator response are the maximum contested exchange. A question must reach the operator early enough for the declared response arrival; feasible timing is not presumed. No second source, new feed or truthful-purpose oracle is added. The paper proposes published investigation ([SRC-LP20-OCT, PDF p5, L218–221](../../../docs/litepaper-2.0.md)); these particular record formats, purpose assertions, disclosure bundles and access arrangements are **not specified by the paper**.

## Individual error tolerances

These qualitative research tolerances preserve [EX-04's historical ceilings](../evidence-self-funded/tolerances.md) in substance, replacing useful SC-23 with S05/S07, SC-07 with S06/S08 and their labelled narrow variants, and extending legitimate protection to S04/S02. Neither niche entry nor discounting relaxes a ceiling. EX-04's September locators remain historical and are not October locators. These are assessment criteria fixed before outcomes, not empirical error rates or approved scoring policy.

**T-FC-N-a — narrow false cuts, reading `a`.** Strict: no cut on legitimate S04/S05/S02/S07 or unmodified S06/S08 merely for self-payment, rewards or missing explanation. For S06-N/S08-N, an adverse finding requires support for the specified representation, falsity, knowledge and eligibility purpose.

**T-MV-N-a — narrow missed violations, reading `a`.** An unresolved alleged `-N` variant may remain unknown when evidence is unavailable within the stated burden. This is an accepted limitation of the inquiry, not evidence of an effective defense. Ignoring evidence that establishes the specified violation is unacceptable.

**T-FC-N-b — narrow false cuts, reading `b`.** Strict at n+7: an explanation-based cut on nonviolating activity is unacceptable for a claim of adequate protection. This includes legitimate S04/S05/S02/S07 and unmodified S06/S08 under the narrow criterion. Earlier adverse exposure may be examined, but correction before claim must be supported, not assumed. Failure to furnish a response is not proof of the specified misrepresentation.

**T-MV-N-b — narrow missed violations, reading `b`.** An established narrow violation receiving full reward is unacceptable. An unexplained case must retain its evidential uncertainty even if this reading permits a cut; penalizing it does not demonstrate that a violation was detected.

**T-FC-B-a — broad false cuts, reading `a`.** Strict: no cut solely for common control, reward responsiveness, unused output or absent public proof of use. Useful production/testing in S05/S07, including mixed-purpose work, must not be classified as reward-only activity without support for the broad allegation. Legitimate outside S04 and ordinary discount routing S02 receive the same protection.

**T-MV-B-a — broad missed violations, reading `a`.** Unresolved funding or purpose may remain unknown within the disclosure limit. Report the resulting exposure as a limitation. An established broad violation left without an adverse consequence is unacceptable.

**T-FC-B-b — broad false cuts, reading `b`.** Strict at n+7: a cut on legitimate S04/S05/S02/S07, including useful work whose operator cannot safely disclose its explanation, exceeds tolerance. Examine that exposure explicitly. A bounded explanation supplied in time must not be rejected merely because useful production/testing also earns rewards.

**T-MV-B-b — broad missed violations, reading `b`.** An established broad violation receiving full reward is unacceptable. A cut for non-explanation is a procedural consequence under this reading, not proof of reward-only purpose or absence of use.

The strict false-cut tolerance reflects the potential reach of the median to the node's whole eligible round reward, including rewards associated with other fees ([SRC-LP20-OCT, PDF p4, L203–208; p6, L271–278](../../../docs/litepaper-2.0.md)). It is a chosen criterion, not a claim that zero error is achievable. Reading `b` can exceed it; the tolerances do not establish the result in advance. Neither a fractional score nor later recovery makes an unsupported cut acceptable by assumption.

## Individual reviewer-effort tolerances

The same ordinal limits apply to both families, both standards and both readings.

**T-ER — routine review, acceptable.** One review of the supplied public records, their sources and arrival times, keeping CL-01 payment, CL-02 execution/delivery, CL-03 correctness, CL-05 independent demand, CL-06 economic value and CL-07 dishonesty distinct. Availability is a separate claim and is not an honesty grade.

**T-EC — bounded contested review, acceptable.** Routine review plus one focused question and one operator response, inspecting one disclosed family source bundle and checking its links to the jobs and stated purpose. Use only the niche production/test-plan/output-use bundle or the discount selection-rule/routing-log/internal-test-plan bundle defined above. The complete review must be feasible by n+7; availability and timing cannot be presumed.

**T-EX — extended investigation, outside tolerance.** Repeated demands, unrelated-job audits, third-party interviews, forensic identity tracing or rebuilding internal systems to establish purpose exceed the effort ceiling.

Under `a`, the additional response is voluntary. Under `b`, the same bounded response is the maximum acceptable burden for this inquiry; an unanswered question still carries that reading's possible cut. No minutes, monetary costs or demonstrated feasibility are assigned. Shared investigation is the paper's intended support (October PDF p5, L218–221), not a measured saving or assumed evidence feed.

## Individual disclosure/privacy tolerance and social burden

**T-DP — selective one-bundle disclosure, acceptable ceiling.** The operator or engineers controlling the material supply a selectively disclosed, redacted excerpt of the stated family bundle, limited to the relevant job references and timing, production/testing objective and linked output use for niche work, or selection rule/routing log and linked internal test plan for discounted work. Revealing limited operational metadata and preparing that response are acceptable research burdens. Unrestricted access to source code, customer payloads, credentials, business accounts, unrelated job histories or a full release roadmap is outside tolerance. If necessary redaction removes the support a judgment needs, retain that limitation; do not assume the excerpt is sufficient. The paper supplies no confidential channel, authenticated internal archive or enforceable access right.

Under `a`, declining disclosure leaves the allegation unresolved unless other obtainable evidence supports it. Under `b`, declining or being unable to disclose can expose even legitimate work to a procedural cut. Evaluate that exposure against the strict false-cut tolerance; privacy protection is not evidence of dishonest purpose. A successful eventual explanation does not erase preparation or disclosure cost.

Validators or shared investigators can raise the question; the operator controls the internal explanation. The paper provides individual scores and a median, mutual accountability and standing to push back (October PDF p4, L193–208), rather than a specified mandatory hearing or guaranteed appeal. Record preparation effort, business disclosure, arrival risk and response access qualitatively. Do not assume public accusations, repeated disputes, preferential treatment, costless correction or a benefit to an accuser. Any permitted procedural cut must be recorded separately from evidence of the selected violation.

## Limits

Synthetic comparisons test whether judgments follow from stated records and assumptions. They do not measure accuracy, reviewer effort, practical access, prevalence, privacy cost or beneficial ownership. Source authenticity, safe adequate redaction, job linkage, response opportunity and timing remain conditions. Missing evidence in these families cannot establish universal impossibility. Validator incentives and the incidence and cost of contested decisions remain for later inquiries. Each tolerance must be assessed individually when outcomes are examined.
