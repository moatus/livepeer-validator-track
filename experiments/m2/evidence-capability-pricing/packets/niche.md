## Limits

This synthetic family tests support for a judgment, not measured accuracy, reviewer effort, prevalence, privacy cost or beneficial ownership. An accurate payment or correct job is not proof of productive purpose, independent demand or subsidy value. The delay offers time for evidence; it does not create evidence or access. Shared investigation has its stated intended role (O-OBS), with no demonstrated savings here. No mandatory hearing, guaranteed appeal, later correction or effect on other nodes is assumed. E2's original pairs remain pending. VD-19 and GT-03 determine whether the distinction is needed; this packet supplies no economics or approved policy.
