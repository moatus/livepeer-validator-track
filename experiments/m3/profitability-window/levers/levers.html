The $10 column is an illustrative materiality screen per earning round, not an abuse threshold. A positive gain includes unsold LPT at the fixed assumed value; it is not realized cash or evidence of misconduct.

Headroom cases artificially move stake shares toward fee shares while keeping reward retention fixed; they do not model a feasible delegation transition. Reward-denial cases assume a positive controlled purchase can cause loss of the entire earning-cohort node reward; the honest no-purchase baseline is unaffected. The assumed probabilities are not findings about validator capability. No principal slashing is modeled.
