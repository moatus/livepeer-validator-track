Model assumptions and reproducibility

Baseline: round 4364; $820.960664 of indexed fees replayed as USDC; LPT valuation $1.71765; scheduled ceiling 29,288.551807 LPT calibrated at hypothetical launch r=0. Node allocation 94%; operator-side fee split 50%; unchanged scores of 1. Stake, commissions and capture-time operator-address ownership proxies stay fixed. Real work is invoiced at $1 per unit with $0.30 execution cost. No setup cost, financing, cash limit, capacity limit, opportunity cost, price feedback or organizational ownership consolidation. Added fees represent hypothetical external demand; recorded baseline settlements do not prove independent demand. Traffic is represented through paid fees, not a measured job-count forecast.

The median is the middle of the 100 individually optimized gains, counting zero when no action pays. These are separate opportunities, not simultaneous actions; they must not be summed as a network attack. LPT gains are valued unsold tokens, not realized cash. Decimal calculations checked all 5,000 selected optima against the M2 accounting engine, reconciled the earlier 100-pool baseline and checked equal-growth scaling for 300 pool/scenario pairs. Exploratory analysis, not an accepted milestone result or observed misconduct.
