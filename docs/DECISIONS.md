# Decision log

Research decisions record the premises the research adopts; they are not protocol decisions and do not change the litepaper. Process decisions about how the work is organised follow; they do not decide any research question.

## Research decisions

- **2026-10-15 — VD-19 research premise for M2.** For M2, rewards are treated as owed to real, correctly executed, honestly represented paid work; buying one's own work solely to raise reward share is treated as dishonest. This is the base reading E2 judges "incorrect result" and dishonesty findings against, and the source of Slice 3's declared criteria for a sufficient-evidence case. It is a research premise, labelled as such in every brief and record that uses it, not an adopted protocol rule. The litepaper leaves the required contribution undefined ([VD-19](../validator-track-workbench.html#VD-19)); that gap stays open and passes to M3 as a candidate litepaper expansion, with the M2 evidence attached showing what each reading would require validators to be able to see. The stricter reading (a contribution beyond the paid work itself) is kept as a conditional branch where a conclusion turns on it. Directed by Shane (@moatus), 2026-10-15.

## Process decisions

- **2026-10-15 — P1 chain-history access.** P1 reads Livepeer history on Arbitrum One from the official Livepeer subgraph on The Graph (subgraph ID `FE63YgkzcpVocxdCEyEYbvjYqEf2kb1A6daMYRxmejYC`) through a free Subgraph Studio key held locally and never committed, printed or echoed. Cross-checks use keyless endpoints: the Arbitrum Foundation public RPC for raw event logs over large block ranges, and the public archive RPC already used by the shared snapshot for historical stake reads at pinned blocks. The default window is 90 days ending at a pinned finalized block; the dated extract, its queries and source hashes are committed under the snapshot's provenance pattern. Two free research reports (Grok 4.7 and GPT-6.1-Sol, 2026-10-15, run logs) agreed on the primary route; the keyed free tiers of Alchemy, QuickNode and Chainstack were rejected because their log queries are capped at 5 to 100 blocks. Directed by Shane (@moatus), 2026-10-15.
