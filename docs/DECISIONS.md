# Research premises

These premises define the research comparisons; they are not adopted protocol rules. Source versions and accepted artifacts are recorded in the [deliverable register](../deliverables/README.md).

- **VD-19 research premise for M2.** For M2, rewards are treated as owed to real, correctly executed, honestly represented paid work; buying one's own work solely to raise reward share is treated as dishonest. This is the base reading E2 judges "incorrect result" and dishonesty findings against, and the source of Slice 3's declared criteria for a sufficient-evidence case. It is a research premise, labelled as such in every brief and record that uses it, not an adopted protocol rule. The litepaper leaves the required contribution undefined ([VD-19](../validator-track-workbench.html#VD-19)); that gap stays open and passes to M3 as a possible litepaper expansion, with the M2 evidence attached showing what each reading would require validators to be able to see. The stricter reading (a contribution beyond the paid work itself) is kept as a conditional branch where a conclusion turns on it.

A changed premise requires the affected comparisons to be reassessed. State the relevant litepaper provision, participant effects, supporting and contrary evidence, and conditions that would change the assessment.
